Usage for future:
=================

Hans Grosse file is free material, as long the credit is given accordingly.
You do so, I will never sue you :P
In fact, my hopes are that many other sprite artists can use these sprites
as base to make more fettgesicht-alike art-ware.
Same goes for my Schabbs, Otto, (mech)Hitler, Gretel Grosse, Fettgesicht,
and Trans Grosse. (And probably future attempts for Barnicle, Uber mutant,
Death Knight, and Angel of Death. But they are not yet been made.)
